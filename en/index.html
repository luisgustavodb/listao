Frequently asked questions

Why is it not on the Play Store?

For now we distribute directly: it is lighter, no middleman, and updates land on the same link. The plan is to hit the Play Store when the app matures.

Is it safe? My phone showed a warning

The warning shows up for any app installed outside the store. The APK is signed, asks only for internet permission and your data stays on your device.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Today Listão is Android — the shared link opens a page with the list for iPhone users.

How do I update later?

Download again from this same link and install on top. Your lists and history stay intact.
